On the exam day

⏰ Reporting & Identity Verification

  • Report 90 minutes before the exam starts.
  • Bring your Admit Card + original photo ID (School ID, Aadhaar Card, Passport, etc.). No photocopies.
  • Leaving before 1:00 PM is prohibited.

🚫 Prohibited Items

  • Banned: papers, books, digital watches, phones.
  • Only simple analog watches are allowed (no memory/connectivity).
  • Possession of a banned item means immediate debarment.

📝 Stationery & Rough Work

  • Rough sheets are provided in the exam hall.
  • Write your Name & Roll Number on the sheets.
  • Return all rough sheets before you leave.

🎒 Allowed Personal Items

  • Bring your own pen and pencil.
  • Recommended: water bottle & snacks.
  • A pocket hand sanitizer is allowed.

Most importantly, have fun! 💥
