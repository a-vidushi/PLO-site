About the Olympiad

PLO is a <- fun, exciting, educational, challenging, puzzling, linguistic -> competition for students studying in grades 6th to 8th (Juniors) and 9th to 12th (Seniors). This competition helps foster problem solving, computational thinking and appreciation for the linguistic diversity and linguistics in general.

Here, you can challenge your mind against puzzles in logic, language and linguistics, from deciphering flower formulae to tracking the evolution of Nna. To actually get on solving these, you don’t need to have any prior knowledge of Linguistics or languages!

Yes! you totally read that right, to know more, continue to What is PLO?

From the Panini Linguistics Olympiad, the top performing students are selected to represent India at the International Linguistics Olympiad (IOL). From the official website:

“The IOL is one of 12 International Science Olympiads for secondary school students, and has been held annually since 2003. Each year, teams of young linguists from around the world gather and test their minds against the world's toughest puzzles in language and linguistics.

No prior knowledge of linguistics or languages is required: even the hardest problems require only your logical ability, patient work, and willingness to think around corners.”

Registration

Registration is available through two tracks based on the medium of school education. Schools can also register a group of students.

Track 1 – Regular (English Medium)

For students whose medium of school education is English.

INR 1,180

INR 1,000 + 18% GST

Track 2 – Access (Regional Language Medium)

For regional-language medium students, English medium students from lower-income families, and JNV students.

INR 354

INR 300 + 18% GST

The supporting documents differ between the tracks, so please read the PLO 2027 registration details before registering.

Important Dates

PLO 2026-2027 will have two major selection rounds on the journey to the International Linguistics Olympiad.

1

Round 1 Registration Opens!

19 September 2026

Registration for the first selection round closes on 31 October 2026. Register now.

2

Round 1 Contest

29 November 2026

A national-level examination for all registered candidates, held at designated centres across India.

3

Round 1 Results Announced!

15 January 2027

Results of the Round 1 contest and the list of students selected for Round 2 are released.

4

Round 2 – APLO

March – April 2027

Top participants from Round 1 take part in the Asia Pacific Linguistics Olympiad against students from around the region.

5

Round 2 – Invitational Camp

May – June 2027

Selected students from across India attend the Invitational Camp in Hyderabad, where they learn linguistics and compete for selection to the International Linguistics Olympiad.

6

Team India at IOL!

21 – 28 July 2027

The selected participants travel to Thailand to compete in the International Linguistics Olympiad 2027!

Finally, What is Linguistics?

Is it about knowing multiple languages? NO.

Is it about knowing how to become more proficient than anyone else in a single language? NO.

Is it about how AI assistants understand us, how shakkar (Hindi and Urdu for sugar) and sugar actually come from the same origin and how a child learns how to speak? YES.
